# Code of Conduct

Hermes welcomes people who participate in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Discuss the work rather than attacking a person.
- Accept that maintainers may ask for a minimal reproduction or decline work
  outside the project's scope.
- Protect private webpages, vault contents, credentials, and security reports.
- Give credit for meaningful contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal abuse.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting credentials, private collected pages, or vulnerability details in a
  public issue.
- Repeated spam, deliberate disruption, or evasion of moderation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this
policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports about
conduct should be sent through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting
form when privacy is required. Do not include unrelated private user data.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the
safety of the project community.
